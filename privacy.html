API applications (advertising data)

Our reporting applications access advertising performance data (such as impressions, clicks, spend and conversion metrics on campaign, ad group and ad level) exclusively from advertising accounts that Fried Ventures GmbH owns or manages with the account holder’s authorization. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in analysing and reporting on advertising we operate (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, where applicable, the performance of contracts with the account holders.

  • The data is aggregated campaign performance data; we do not collect personal profiles of platform members through these applications.
  • Data is stored in Google BigQuery in the European Union (region: europe-west3, Frankfurt).
  • Data is used solely for reporting and analysis for the respective account holder. We do not sell data and do not share it with third parties beyond the processors named here.
  • Data is retained for as long as needed for reporting purposes and deleted upon request of the account holder.
